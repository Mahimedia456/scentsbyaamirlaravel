# Scents by Aamir — Phase 01 Fragrances / Catalogue Image Prompt

## Image 01 — Fragrance Wardrobe Hero

**Bottle reference required:** NO

**Exact filename:** `fragrances-hero.webp`

**Save to:**
`public/images/catalog/fragrances-hero.webp`

**Recommended output:** 2400 × 1200 landscape, WebP after generation/export.

### Prompt

Create an extraordinary high-fashion fragrance wardrobe hero artwork for the luxury perfume house Scents by Aamir.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO people.
NO text.
NO logo.
NO watermark.

The image should express a complete fragrance wardrobe through raw material, light, shadow and texture rather than through a product bottle.

Build a restrained sculptural composition primarily across the RIGHT 50% of the frame:

- a curved piece of dark natural oud wood with rich grain,
- one translucent amber resin stone,
- a small cluster of deep burgundy rose petals,
- a narrow fresh bergamot peel,
- a few pale sandalwood curls,
- one delicate white floral stem,
- a very subtle ribbon of smoke travelling upward behind the materials,
- matte black mineral stone and a small reflective wet-stone detail.

The entire LEFT 42–45% must remain dark, calm and relatively empty so the website can place the catalogue headline and CTA over it.

Lighting:
controlled directional gallery lighting,
rich black and charcoal shadows,
subtle champagne-gold highlights,
small cool highlights around the fresh materials,
realistic tactile texture,
no overexposed areas.

Mood:
world-class niche fragrance editorial,
architectural,
minimal,
tactile,
modern,
quiet luxury,
expensive without looking ornamental.

Avoid a literal ingredient flat-lay. Treat every material like a sculptural object in a museum exhibition.

2400x1200 landscape.

## Placement / Crop Rules

Desktop: hero is full-width under the existing Scents by Aamir header. Text sits on the left, so keep the strongest visual weight on the right.

Mobile: the image uses `object-cover`; keep important materials near the center-right rather than at the extreme right edge so the crop still works.

## Product Grid Images

No new generated images are required for the catalogue cards. Phase 01 intentionally uses the real product images already imported into Laravel/WooCommerce migration data. This keeps the catalogue authentic and avoids repeating campaign banners between every product row.
