# Scents by Aamir — Phase 04 Product Detail Image Prompts

Phase 04 is product-specific. Do **not** create generic images once and reuse them across every product.

For each product, create a folder using its Laravel product slug:

`public/images/products/{product-slug}/`

Example:

`public/images/products/smoky-chic/`

The Blade file automatically looks for these exact optional files:

- `campaign-world.webp`
- `notes.webp`
- `ritual.webp`

If any file is missing, the product page safely falls back to the uploaded/imported product gallery.

---

# 1. `campaign-world.webp`

**Purpose:** large editorial scent-world section  
**Recommended size:** 2000 × 1600 landscape/near-square  
**Bottle:** YES — upload the exact bottle for THAT product

## Master prompt

Create a world-class niche fragrance campaign for Scents by Aamir using the exact uploaded bottle of [PRODUCT NAME].

PRESERVE exactly:
the bottle silhouette,
cap,
glass,
liquid color,
Scents by Aamir logo,
label,
product name,
proportions.

Do not redesign the product.
Do not copy another fragrance brand's bottle, packaging or advertising.

Build the visual world around this product's actual scent identity:

Fragrance character: [MEN / WOMEN / UNISEX]
Main notes/materials: [INSERT REAL NOTES]
Mood: [INSERT PRODUCT MOOD]

Position the bottle on the RIGHT-CENTER or CENTER of the composition, integrated into a sculptural fragrance environment rather than floating as a catalogue cutout.

Use only a restrained selection of materials relevant to this exact fragrance.
Treat ingredients as precious editorial objects.

Lighting:
cinematic directional light,
deep controlled shadow,
realistic premium glass reflections,
subtle champagne / bronze accents appropriate to the fragrance,
high-end commercial fragrance photography.

Composition must still have useful negative space and should feel like an editorial chapter on a luxury website.

No people.
No added text.
No extra brand logos.
No watermark.
No fake product packaging.

2000x1600.

### Examples of art direction by product

- **Dark Seduction:** coffee bean, white floral, vanilla, amber glow; dark gourmand; bottle YES.
- **Smoky Chic:** charred wood, smoke, resin, mineral stone; bottle YES.
- **Le Reve Dore:** pale floral, translucent pear-like detail, champagne stone, warm ivory-gold light; bottle YES.
- **Floral Charm:** dark burgundy + pale petals, soft green stem, mineral stone; bottle YES.
- **Ocean Spirit:** wet obsidian, citrus peel, white blossom, restrained water texture; bottle YES.
- **Desert Soul:** dry oud, resin, black stone, subtle nocturnal mineral atmosphere; bottle YES.
- **Dark Aure:** oud, dark rose, resin, restrained smoke; bottle YES.
- **Amerel:** elegant pale floral structure, warm polished light, quiet stone surface; bottle YES.

# 2. `notes.webp`

**Purpose:** visual companion inside Notes tab  
**Recommended size:** 1400 × 1600 portrait  
**Bottle:** NO

## Master prompt

Create a high-fashion raw-material portrait representing the note structure of [PRODUCT NAME] by Scents by Aamir.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO text.
NO people.
NO watermark.

Use only the REAL principal materials of this fragrance:

Opening: [TOP / OPENING MATERIALS]
Heart: [HEART MATERIALS]
Dry-down: [BASE MATERIALS]

Arrange a restrained selection of 3–5 materials like museum objects.
Do not build a kitchen flat-lay.
Do not label the ingredients.

Use matte black, warm grey or ivory mineral stone depending on the fragrance identity.
Use directional editorial lighting with rich realistic textures and controlled negative space.

The image must communicate texture, temperature and progression rather than literally showing a perfume pyramid.

1400x1600 portrait.

# 3. `ritual.webp`

**Purpose:** House Ritual / wearing section  
**Recommended size:** 1800 × 1600  
**Bottle:** Usually NO

For most products this should be bottle-free so the page does not repeat the bottle again.

## Master prompt — NO bottle

Create a quiet luxury editorial artwork about the ritual of wearing [PRODUCT NAME].

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO text.
NO logo.
NO watermark.

Do not show a full identifiable person.

Create an intimate close-up atmosphere using one of:
a cropped wrist / collarbone / fabric fold without a visible face,
a dark tailored sleeve,
a silk or cotton texture,
soft skin-toned light,
a subtle atmospheric shadow,
or an abstract tactile composition inspired by application to pulse points.

The image should communicate:
skin,
proximity,
movement,
personal ritual,
and fragrance settling over time.

Keep the treatment sophisticated, non-sexual, editorial and timeless.

Use lighting and material tones appropriate to [PRODUCT MOOD].

1800x1600.

### Alternative fully object-based ritual prompt

If you do not want any human skin:

Create a minimalist luxury fragrance ritual still-life with a folded premium fabric, a single blotter strip, a small clear glass dropper, soft directional light and one relevant raw fragrance material.

NO perfume bottle.
NO readable text.
NO people.
NO packaging.
No watermark.

1800x1600.

# Bottle usage summary

| File | Bottle? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Main imported/uploaded product gallery | YES | Actual commerce/product photography |
| `campaign-world.webp` | YES | One premium product campaign moment |
| `notes.webp` | NO | Raw materials / note storytelling |
| `ritual.webp` | NO preferred | Avoid repetitive bottle use |

This creates the correct rhythm:
**commerce bottle → composition/materials → one campaign bottle → material links → ritual without bottle → related products.**
