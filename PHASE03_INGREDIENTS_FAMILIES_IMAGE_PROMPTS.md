## A. Main Ingredients Hero

**Filename:** `ingredients-hero.webp`  
**Size:** 2400 × 1200  
**Bottle:** NO

### Prompt

Create an extraordinary high-fashion raw fragrance materials hero for the luxury perfume house Scents by Aamir.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO people.
NO text.
NO logo.
NO watermark.

Compose a sculptural material landscape mainly across the RIGHT 52%:

dark natural oud wood with rich grain,
one deep burgundy rose,
translucent amber resin,
fresh bergamot,
pale sandalwood curls,
one delicate white jasmine flower,
a dark vanilla pod,
and a very restrained cluster of dry spices.

Arrange the materials like precious museum objects on matte black mineral stone.

A single fine ribbon of smoke moves behind the composition.

The entire LEFT 42% must remain deep black and uncluttered for website title, description and buttons.

Lighting:
directional gallery light,
controlled champagne-gold highlights,
small clean fresh highlights around citrus and jasmine,
deep charcoal shadow,
hyper-real tactile textures.

Modern niche perfumery.
Architectural.
Quiet luxury.
No kitchen flat-lay look.

2400x1200 landscape.

## B. Fragrance Families Hero

**Filename:** `families-hero.webp`  
**Size:** 2200 × 1100  
**Bottle:** NO

### Prompt

Create a minimalist abstract fragrance-family artwork for a world-class luxury perfume website.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO text.
NO people.

Use a black-to-charcoal architectural background.

Across the RIGHT half, create six subtle material zones that blend naturally into one editorial composition:

fresh citrus peel and water,
deep rose petals,
dry pale wood,
dark oud,
translucent amber resin,
and warm spices.

Do not separate them into boxes.
The materials should transition through light, shadow and texture like one continuous fragrance spectrum.

Keep LEFT 42% almost empty and dark for typography.

Museum-style lighting.
Deep black.
Warm champagne highlights.
One tiny cool mineral reflection.
Highly realistic materials.

2200x1100 landscape.

# Ingredient cards / detail heroes

All are **NO BOTTLE**.

## 1. Oud
**Filename:** `oud-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a sculptural macro still-life of rare dark oud wood on matte black mineral stone.
Show deep natural grain, resin-darkened edges and one tiny translucent resin fragment.
A very thin smoke line in the background.
Gallery lighting, black charcoal environment, restrained bronze highlights.
No bottle, no packaging, no text, no people, no watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 2. Rose
**Filename:** `rose-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a dark luxury botanical portrait of one deep burgundy rose and several loose velvet petals on black mineral stone.
The flower must look natural, rich and editorial rather than bridal.
Very subtle moisture on selected petals.
Deep black background with restrained champagne rim light.
No bottle, no packaging, no text, no people, no watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 3. Amber
**Filename:** `amber-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a museum-like macro photograph of translucent amber resin stones placed on dark mineral rock.
Show realistic internal inclusions and warm golden light passing through the resin.
Most of the frame remains deep charcoal and black.
No jewelry styling.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 4. Citrus
**Filename:** `citrus-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a sophisticated fresh-fragrance ingredient portrait.
One dark-green bergamot or refined citrus fruit, partially cut, on wet graphite stone.
A long sculptural curl of peel and a few precise water droplets.
Black-charcoal environment with controlled clean highlights.
Not tropical, not bright commercial food photography.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 5. Sandalwood
**Filename:** `sandalwood-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a quiet luxury still-life of pale sandalwood.
Use natural creamy wood curls and one smooth cut piece on warm grey mineral stone.
Soft directional light, cream/champagne highlights, deep controlled shadow.
Architectural and tactile.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 6. Jasmine
**Filename:** `jasmine-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create an elegant botanical fragrance portrait using a restrained cluster of fresh white jasmine flowers.
Place them on nearly black stone with a few dark green stems.
Soft ivory petals illuminated by narrow directional light.
Deep background, delicate realistic texture, modern editorial mood.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 7. Vanilla
**Filename:** `vanilla-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a sophisticated perfume-material portrait of natural dark vanilla pods.
Arrange two or three curved pods on warm black mineral stone with a very subtle cream-toned powder or botanical texture nearby.
Warm restrained light, deep shadows, sensual but minimal.
Do not make it look like baking photography.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.

## 8. Spices
**Filename:** `spices-ingredient.webp`
**Size:** 1600 × 1800 portrait

Create a high-fashion fragrance spice composition on matte black stone.
Use a very restrained selection of cardamom, black pepper, saffron threads and one cinnamon fragment.
Treat each ingredient like a museum object with large negative space.
Warm bronze highlights and deep black shadows.
Not a kitchen spice flat-lay.
No bottle, packaging, people, text or watermark.
1600x1800 portrait.
