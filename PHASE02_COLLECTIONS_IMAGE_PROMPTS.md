## 1. Collections Landing Hero

**Filename:** `collections-hero.webp`  
**Size:** 2400 × 1200 landscape  
**Bottle reference required:** NO

### Prompt

Create a world-class luxury fragrance wardrobe hero artwork for the Scents by Aamir collections page.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.
NO people.
NO text.
NO logo.
NO watermark.

Build an architectural still-life that represents several fragrance personalities within one visual world.

On the RIGHT 50–55%, arrange a restrained composition of:
dark natural oud wood,
translucent amber resin,
one deep burgundy rose,
a narrow curl of fresh bergamot peel,
pale sandalwood,
one delicate white flower,
black mineral stone,
a small reflective wet-stone surface,
and a very fine ribbon of smoke.

The materials should feel like sculptural museum objects rather than ingredients on a table.

The entire LEFT 42% must remain deep black / charcoal and visually quiet for website typography and CTA buttons.

Lighting:
directional gallery lighting,
deep controlled shadows,
champagne-gold highlights,
a tiny cool highlight around fresh materials,
rich tactile detail.

Mood:
editorial,
architectural,
modern niche perfumery,
quiet luxury,
timeless,
high fashion.

2400x1200 landscape.

## 2. Signature Worlds

**Filename:** `signature-worlds.webp`  
**Size:** 2000 × 1600  
**Bottle reference required:** YES — upload 2 or 3 of your real Scents by Aamir bottle references.

### Prompt

Create a premium editorial campaign representing the Scents by Aamir Signature Worlds collection.

Use ONLY the exact Scents by Aamir perfume bottle references I upload.

Preserve each bottle's actual silhouette, cap, logo, label, proportions and product identity.
Do not invent new bottle designs.
Do not copy another perfume brand.

Use only TWO OR THREE bottles maximum.

Arrange them as an elegant fragrance wardrobe on staggered matte-black mineral plinths.
The bottles should not be lined up like an online catalogue.

Add extremely restrained supporting materials:
one dark wood fragment,
one translucent amber resin stone,
one pale flower.

Deep black environment.
Controlled champagne-gold highlights.
Soft sculptural shadows.
Subtle depth and atmosphere.

Leave useful darker negative space in the upper-left and lower-left areas for website typography.

Mood:
defining house signatures,
confident,
timeless,
polished,
editorial luxury.

No people.
No added text.
No watermark.

2000x1600.

## 3. Nocturnal Collection

**Filename:** `nocturnal.webp`  
**Size:** 1800 × 1200 landscape  
**Bottle reference required:** NO

### Prompt

Create a cinematic nocturnal fragrance artwork for the luxury perfume house Scents by Aamir.

NO perfume bottle.

Build a sculptural arrangement on nearly black mineral stone:
charred natural oud wood,
dark resin,
one small translucent amber stone,
a restrained leather-like dark texture,
and a thin elegant ribbon of incense smoke.

In the distance, allow a barely perceptible cool moonlit mineral texture to dissolve into darkness.

Lighting:
deep black and charcoal,
warm bronze edge lighting around the raw materials,
very restrained cool moonlight,
high contrast but with visible texture in the shadows.

The image must feel dry, smoky, intimate and expensive.

Do not show:
fire,
desert palaces,
people,
bottles,
packaging,
text,
logos.

Keep the lower-left region usable for website copy.

1800x1200 landscape.

## 4. Light Studies Collection

**Filename:** `light-studies.webp`  
**Size:** 1800 × 1200 landscape  
**Bottle reference required:** NO

### Prompt

Create an artistic luxury fragrance study expressing brightness, skin, citrus and clean air.

NO perfume bottle.
NO packaging.

Use a warm ivory limestone surface with generous negative space.

Create a minimal sculptural arrangement on the RIGHT half:
one freshly cut bergamot,
a curled translucent citrus peel,
one delicate white blossom,
pale smooth wood,
a small clear mineral crystal,
and several realistic water droplets.

A very subtle sheer cream fabric may enter from the far right edge.

Soft directional daylight.
Warm ivory, mineral white, pale champagne and restrained natural green.
Extremely clean shadows and realistic textures.

The image should feel:
luminous,
airy,
skin-like,
fresh,
modern,
quietly luxurious.

No tropical beach.
No large water splash.
No people.
No text.
No logo.
No watermark.

1800x1200 landscape.
